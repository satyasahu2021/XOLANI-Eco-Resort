# DECISION LOG

- DEC-000001: GitHub repository adopted.
- DEC-000002: Bootstrap structure frozen.

- DEC-000003: Hybrid DPR accommodation mix approved - 8 cottage keys comprising 6 Standard Family Cottages based on AST-041 and 2 Premium Couple Cottages based on AST-043. AST-042 is rejected as a duplicate and is not approved for publication. AST-043 is retained as the premium-cottage design basis and is not treated as a supersession of AST-041. The A-004 drawing-identity conflict remains subject to controlled document-resolution; no drawing number is to be silently renumbered or overwritten. This accommodation mix shall be used as the working Hybrid DPR basis for subsequent accommodation scheduling, area planning, CAPEX, utilities, staffing and revenue modelling.

- DEC-000004: Premium Couple Cottage one-bedroom design concept approved as the working Hybrid DPR design basis for 2 Premium Couple Cottage keys. The approved concept is an independent new accommodation product and does not modify, supersede, or replace AST-041, which remains the approved two-bedroom Standard Family Cottage basis for 6 keys. The Base Master Plan and existing Plot 4/C-01 to C-08 cottage arrangement remain unchanged. The project compass convention remains North down, South up, East left and West right. The current West-side entrance and East-side private deck arrangement are retained as part of the approved design concept. The private deck remains based on the existing standard cottage deck size. Premium differentiation shall be achieved through the approved one-bedroom arrangement, king bed, premium bathroom with rain shower and vanity, mini-bar/beverage pantry, couple lounge, premium FF&E and lighting. The current concept dimensions and presented area schedule are accepted as the working design-study basis. No new AST number or drawing number is assigned by this decision. The concept remains subject to subsequent technical coordination, costing, MEP development, DAR preparation and controlled drawing identification before construction or publication.
