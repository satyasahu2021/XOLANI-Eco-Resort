## Orientation

The compass printed on AST-000047 indicates:

- North: UP
- South: DOWN
- East: RIGHT
- West: LEFT

The locked XOLANI project convention is:

- North: DOWN
- South: UP
- East: LEFT
- West: RIGHT

Accordingly, AST-000047 contains an **orientation representation discrepancy**.

No correction has been applied to the source PNG at this stage.

Any approved correction must be limited strictly to the controlled compass representation.
