## Project Master-Plan Disposition

**REJECTED — NOT APPROVED AS CURRENT PROJECT MASTER PLAN / DESIGN BASIS**

AST-000049 / A-010 is rejected as the current project master plan.

The rejection applies to the master-plan design represented by the drawing, including its current site-planning arrangement and associated facility positioning.

The drawing shall not be treated as the controlling basis for the future project master plan.

## Rejection Basis

The project master-plan design represented by A-010 is not accepted as the established project design direction.

Accordingly, the drawing shall not be used as the current controlling basis for:

- Site master planning
- Facility positioning
- Cottage cluster planning
- Internal circulation planning
- Landscape planning
- Parking planning
- Fire-drive planning
- Service-yard planning
- Basement-ramp planning
- Publication master-plan drawings

The rejection of A-010 does not constitute rejection of the XOLANI project itself or of individual facilities that may have independent controlled design records.
