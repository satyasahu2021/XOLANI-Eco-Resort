## Rejection Basis

AST-000052 is rejected because:

1. It explicitly derives its arrangement from the **locked master development plan**.
2. The referenced master-plan direction represented by AST-000049 / A-010 has been rejected.
3. The section key plan reproduces the rejected master-development arrangement.
4. The cottage section is presented generically as **C-01 to C-08 Eco Luxury Cottages** rather than establishing the controlling **2 Premium + 6 2-BHK Family** accommodation mix.
5. The orientation representation differs from the controlled project orientation convention.

These issues prevent AST-000052 from being accepted as a current controlled project design basis.
