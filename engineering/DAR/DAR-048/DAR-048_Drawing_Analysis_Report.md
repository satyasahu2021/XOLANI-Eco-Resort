## Controlled Disposition

**REJECTED — NOT APPROVED FOR PROJECT DESIGN BASIS / PUBLICATION**

AST-000048 / A-009 is rejected as a controlling project accommodation design basis.

The source drawing depicts a generic Typical Eco Cottage configuration and states **08 NOS.** cottages. Although the source count corresponds numerically with the total eight-cottage project program, the source does not establish the approved product mix of:

- 6 Standard Family Cottages based on AST-041; and
- 2 Premium Couple Cottages based on AST-043.

Accordingly, AST-000048 shall not be used to introduce an alternative cottage design basis or to replace either AST-041 or AST-043.

## Rejection Basis

The rejection is based on controlled project design governance, specifically the established Hybrid DPR accommodation decision.

The approved project basis remains:

**2 Premium Couple Cottages + 6 Standard Family Cottages = 8 total cottage keys.**

AST-000048 is therefore retained only as source/historical drawing evidence and is not approved for publication or adoption as a current project design basis.
