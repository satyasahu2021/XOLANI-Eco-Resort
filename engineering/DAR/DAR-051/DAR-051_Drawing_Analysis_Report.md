## Controlled Disposition

**REJECTED — NOT APPROVED AS CURRENT PROJECT DESIGN BASIS / PUBLICATION**

AST-000051 / A-010 is rejected as the current controlling building-elevation design basis.

The rejection applies to the elevation package as a current project design-basis/publication document.

The drawing shall not be used as the controlling basis for:

- Building elevations
- Cottage elevations
- Facility elevation standards
- Master-plan-derived architectural publication
- Cottage product differentiation
- Current project architectural design basis

The rejection does not constitute rejection of the individual facilities or of the XOLANI project itself where those facilities have independent controlled design records.

## Rejection Basis

AST-000051 is rejected because:

1. It explicitly derives its arrangement from the **locked master development plan**.
2. The corresponding master-plan direction represented by AST-000049 / A-010 has been rejected.
3. The cottage elevations are presented generically as **C-01 to C-08 Eco Luxury Cottages** rather than establishing the controlling **2 Premium + 6 2-BHK Family** accommodation mix.
4. The drawing number **A-010** conflicts with the previously identified AST-000049 / A-010 master plan.
5. The orientation representation differs from the controlled project orientation convention.

These issues prevent AST-000051 from being accepted as a current controlled project design basis.
